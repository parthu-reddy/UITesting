# Confirm role scope before changing a product to satisfy an E2E plan

Treat an E2E plan as a reviewable specification. Check each scenario against the actor’s responsibilities and current product requirements before adding controls, exposing backend data, or declaring an absent control a defect. A rider verifies and delivers the assigned package; itemized contents are not required unless a separate product requirement establishes that responsibility.

When a scenario is unnecessary, correct the plan and assertions, document the decision, and retain existing test evidence. Do not expand service payloads merely to make an unnecessary test pass.
