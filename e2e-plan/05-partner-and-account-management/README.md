# 05-partner-and-account-management

Cover supporting workflows using seeded accounts and reversible changes.

Work through one functionality folder at a time:

- [profiles-settings-and-kyc](profiles-settings-and-kyc/README.md): Profiles, settings, onboarding and KYC; reuse existing records by default.
- [restaurant-outlets-and-menu-management](restaurant-outlets-and-menu-management/README.md): Brand/outlet management, restaurant settings and menu stock.
- [campaigns-and-promotions](campaigns-and-promotions/README.md): Campaigns, advertisements and promotion management.
- [rider-wallet-earnings-and-history](rider-wallet-earnings-and-history/README.md): Rider wallet, earnings and delivery history.
- [restaurant-earnings](restaurant-earnings/README.md): Restaurant earnings and reporting.
- [business-wallet](business-wallet/scenarios.md): The organisation's business wallet — access, top-up and spend, the wallet page (Business Platform W1–W3).
- [ads-manager](ads-manager/scenarios.md): Ad account per organisation and the Ads Manager (Business Platform A1–A4); supersedes campaigns-and-promotions at A4.
